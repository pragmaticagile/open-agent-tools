# Role Playbooks

Use these playbooks to tune the same Azure DevOps facts for different audiences.

## Team Member

Goal: help the person focus, unblock, and improve.

Answer:

- Top 3 focus items for today.
- Assigned work by state, priority, severity, and iteration.
- Blocked or waiting items.
- Work aging in active, review, test, or blocked states.
- Items closest to sprint, feature, epic, or Delivery Plan goal.
- What to update in Azure DevOps today.
- Improvement insight from recent work, such as repeated carryover, unclear stories, oversized items, missing estimates, or frequent blockers.

Avoid management tone. Be practical and specific.

## Product Owner / Product Manager

Goal: protect value, clarity, and roadmap confidence.

Answer:

- Goal/roadmap signal from epics, features, Delivery Plans, tags, iterations, areas, and target dates when available.
- Backlog health: unrefined, unestimated, unassigned, stale, duplicate, low-clarity, missing acceptance criteria, or missing parent items.
- Priority and scope movement.
- Release and Delivery Plan readiness.
- Customer/value risks and tradeoff options.

If Delivery Plans are blocked, say so and fall back to epics, features, tags, area paths, iteration paths, target dates, and sprint goals.

## Delivery Lead / Scrum Master

Goal: keep sprint execution healthy.

Answer:

- Sprint goal and delivery confidence.
- Completed, in progress, not started, blocked, and bug work.
- Capacity and ownership gaps.
- Dependencies, handoff risks, and aging blockers.
- Daily team action list.
- Process improvement: WIP limits, split work, escalation, review/test flow, blocker aging, or estimate hygiene.

## Senior Manager

Goal: fast delivery signal and decisions.

Answer:

- Overall confidence: Green, Amber, or Red with reason.
- Major progress since last update when data exists.
- Main delivery risks and business impact.
- Decisions or escalations needed.
- Date, target, release, Delivery Plan, or goal risks.
- No raw Azure DevOps dumps unless requested.

## PMO

Goal: governance clarity and action tracking.

Answer:

- Portfolio/project status summary.
- Delivery Plan, milestone, target date, and release risks.
- Aging work items, missing owners, stale blockers, overdue items, and missing estimates.
- Dependency register.
- Action register with owner, due date, and next update.
- Reporting caveats and data quality gaps.
