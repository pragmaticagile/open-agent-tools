# Role Playbooks

Use these playbooks to tune the same Jira facts for different audiences.

## Team Member

Goal: help the person focus, unblock, and improve.

Answer:

- Top 3 focus items for today.
- Assigned work by status and priority.
- Blocked or waiting items.
- Work aging in progress or review.
- Items closest to sprint/project goal.
- What to update in Jira today.
- Improvement insight from recent work, such as repeated carryover, unclear stories, or frequent blockers.

Avoid management tone. Be practical and specific.

## Product Owner / Product Manager

Goal: protect value, clarity, and roadmap confidence.

Answer:

- Goal/roadmap signal from epics, versions, labels, releases, and Plans when available.
- Backlog health: unrefined, unestimated, unassigned, stale, duplicate, or low-clarity items.
- Priority and scope movement.
- Release readiness and open product decisions.
- Customer/value risks and tradeoff options.

If Plans/Roadmaps are blocked, say so and fall back to epics, fix versions, components, labels, and sprint goals.

## Delivery Lead / Scrum Master

Goal: keep sprint execution healthy.

Answer:

- Sprint goal and delivery confidence.
- Completed, in progress, not started, blocked, and bug work.
- Capacity and ownership gaps.
- Dependencies and handoff risks.
- Daily team action list.
- Process improvement: WIP limits, split work, escalation, review flow, or blocker aging.

## Senior Manager

Goal: fast delivery signal and decisions.

Answer:

- Overall confidence: green, amber, or red with reason.
- Major progress since last update.
- Main delivery risks and business impact.
- Decisions or escalations needed.
- Date/release/goal risks.
- No raw Jira dumps unless requested.

## PMO

Goal: governance clarity and action tracking.

Answer:

- Portfolio/project status summary.
- Milestone and release risks.
- Aging issues, missing owners, stale blockers, overdue items.
- Dependency register.
- Action register with owner, due date, and next update.
- Reporting caveats and data quality gaps.
